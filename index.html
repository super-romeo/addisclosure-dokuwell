How it works

In the UK, advertising by creators has to be obviously identifiable as advertising. The CAP Code section 2 and the guidance of the Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) ask for a clear label such as #ad placed upfront, where people will see it before they engage with the content.

Since the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024, the Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) can also act directly against unfair commercial practices, and hidden advertising is one of them. For a brand or an agency, a missed label is a risk for every post of a campaign.

Most checks happen after a post is live, when the mistake is already public. Ad Label Check works earlier: the creator sends the draft, the service checks the caption, the first frame of a video and the first seconds of audio, and the post is approved or returned with what to fix.

Each check is stored with its date, the draft and the result. When a client or a regulator asks how a campaign was checked, you open the campaign and show the record of every post.

The service is for in-house marketing teams and agencies with several clients and creator rosters. You stay responsible for your campaigns; the service only checks drafts and keeps the records.
